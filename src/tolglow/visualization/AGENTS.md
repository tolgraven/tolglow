# Presentation sampling

Keep effect previews over already committed immutable snapshots. Sample on
demand from a mounted surface, share predictions across readers and release
them with the last reader. Use the registered scalar effect compiler and
fixture resolution for native colors. Keep device protocols in Afterglow and
place websocket/Reagent bindings in Studio. Cover cue isolation, timing,
ownership and the live-effect throttle ordering in
`test/tolglow/visualization/`. See `docs/cue-pad-previews.md`.
